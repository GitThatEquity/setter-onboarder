# First Day Plan

The day in order. Give the new hire one block at a time. Do not paste this whole file. Ask what they already know and skip what they have.

The exact clock times, who they meet, and when they first dial are set by the owner. This file gives the order of learning, not a schedule. If they ask for times, see `12_not_covered.md`.

## Block 1: Settle in (first 15 minutes)

- Say hello. Say day one is for learning, not performing.
- Ask what they already know: sales, real estate, foreclosure, phones.
- Tell them the one-sentence job: have a kind conversation with a homeowner and, if it makes sense, set an appointment with a closer.

## Block 2: Why we do this (read `03_why_we_do_this.md`)

- The mission and the one rule: "How can I serve this person?" not "How can I get this deal?"
- This comes before any script, because it changes how every call sounds.

## Block 3: What a setter does (read `02_the_role.md`, then `13_pay_and_hours.md` if they ask)

- A normal day, what success looks like, what is not their job.
- Many new hires worry about pay early. If they ask, answer honestly from `13_pay_and_hours.md`: a percentage of the assignment fee paid at closing, 2 hours of calls a day, 5 days a week, flexible timing. Do not promise income.

Also tell them where they start: **in the pool, calling all the numbers.** Once they show they are doing the work, being consistent, and putting in the time, they move up: into a township and county group, and eventually to a higher percentage. Once they build rapport with a homeowner, that lead becomes theirs.

## Block 4: Just enough foreclosure (read `04_foreclosure_basics.md`)

- The timeline in plain English, and the words they will hear: NOD, NOTS, sheriff sale, reinstatement, Act 91.
- They do not need to master it. They need to not be surprised.

## Block 5: How a lead reaches you (read `06_lead_stages_and_tiers.md` and `07_tools_and_workflow.md`)

- NOD vs NOTS, Gold / Silver / Bronze / Pending.
- The path a lead takes, at a high level.

## Block 6: The call (read `14_setter_scripts.md` Beginner script, then `08_call_framework_and_objections.md`)

- Show only the Beginner script. Opener, 3 qualifying questions, then book. Hold the Experienced and Cold Call scripts for later in the week.
- Role-play the opener with the onboarder as the homeowner (made-up details only).
- The 4-step objection pattern.
- The 3 core objections. Role-play each one, with the onboarder as the homeowner (made-up details only).

## Block 7: Boundaries and who to ask (read `09_boundaries_and_escalation.md` and `10_whos_who.md`)

- What they never do, and when they hand off.
- Who owns which questions.

## Hold for later in the week

Do not bring these up on day one unless they ask:

- `05_reading_the_county_list.md` (the sheriff sale list columns)
- Equity, deal structures, Subject-To, seller finance details
- Follow-up tracks and long-term check-ins
- Anything about the automation pipeline behind the leads

## End of day check

Ask: "What's one thing that still feels fuzzy?" Answer that one. Then name one thing they should ask the owner tomorrow that this folder cannot answer (see `12_not_covered.md`).
