# Lead Stages and Tiers

## Stages

NOD and NOTS are our **internal lead tags**, not legal terms. In Pennsylvania, a foreclosure ends in a **sheriff sale**, not a trustee sale. Never say "NOD" or "NOTS" to a homeowner. Say "foreclosure" and "sheriff sale."

| Stage | Meaning | Campaign approach |
|---|---|---|
| NOD | The homeowner is in foreclosure but there is no sheriff sale date yet. | Educate, build trust, clarify options |
| NOTS | A sheriff sale date is set. | Countdown urgency, run to the date |
| Expired | A sale was scheduled and then stopped. | Long-term check-ins, status follow-up |

## Tiers (priority)

| Tier | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| GOLD | NOTS, 30 days or less to sale, individual owner, good address | Call/door immediately. Highest urgency. |
| SILVER | NOTS 31 to 90 days, or strong NOD signals | Active outreach, multi-touch |
| BRONZE | NOD, standard, no imminent sale date | Regular cadence outreach |
| PENDING | Flagged for manual review (trust, estate, probate, ambiguous) | Human review before outreach |

How to say it simply to a new hire: Gold is "call now," Silver is "actively working," Bronze is "steady check-ins," Pending is "don't touch until someone reviews it."

## Lead status tags

Used in the tracking system:

Fresh, Knocked, No Answer, Contacted, Follow Up, Hot, Dead.
Also: Do Not Contact, Resolved, Sold, Manual Review.

What the working ones mean (from the follow-up guidance):

- **Fresh / New**: not contacted yet.
- **Contact Attempted / No Answer**: tried but did not speak to them. Vary the method and follow up every few days depending on how close the sale date is.
- **Contacted / Conversation Started**: spoke, but not enough information yet.
- **Follow Up / Warm**: open to talking and may need help. Follow up within 24 hours, aim to set an appointment.
- **Hot**: sale date is close, homeowner is responsive, a real solution may exist. Same-day follow-up.
- **Dead**: not interested, no path, already resolved, wrong property, or asked not to be contacted. Stop outreach.
- **Do Not Contact**: they asked to stop. Always honored.
- **Manual Review**: needs a human decision first.

## Campaign types

| Campaign | Trigger | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| NOTS Stage Track | Days to sale 30 or less | Urgency and options before the sale |
| NOD Stage Track | New NOD filed | Educate and build the relationship |
| Expired Stage Track | Sale date passed | Long-term check-in |
| Follow Up Track | After BK, mod, reinstatement, or probate | Stay top of mind |
| Objection Track | Seller states a specific belief | A specific 10 to 14 day sequence |

New hires do not need to memorize the tracks on day one. Know that they exist.
