# Who's Who

This tells the onboarder who owns what. Only roles listed here may be named. Names and contact details for people other than the owner are not in this folder. If a new hire asks for one, say it is not in your folder and tell them to ask the owner.

## People and roles

| Role | Who | Owns | Send these questions here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner / founder | Michael Amad | Your exact pay rate, the JV agreement (the general structure is in `13_pay_and_hours.md`), access and logins, scripts, deal structures, final offers, ethical decisions, anything unusual | Pay, hours, contract, access, "I'm not sure what to do," a distressed homeowner, anything this folder can't answer |
| Closer | Set per appointment by the owner. Name not in this folder. | The appointment with the homeowner: the numbers, the options, the offer or contract | A homeowner question that needs numbers, an offer, or legal-adjacent detail |
| Advisor | Assigned by the owner. Name not in this folder. | The advisor call: a phone conversation with the homeowner after a setter books it | Questions about what happens on an advisor call |
| Door knocker / field team | Assigned by the owner. Names not in this folder. | The in-person sit-down: they go out and meet the homeowner. Door knockers normally do these | Questions about what happens at an in-person visit |
| Other setters | Name(s) not in this folder | Peer support on day-to-day calling | Ask the owner who to shadow |

## Outside resources a setter may point homeowners toward (not teammates)

These are categories, not named organizations. Do not make up names or numbers.

- A licensed Pennsylvania attorney or legal aid organization, for legal questions.
- A HUD-approved housing counselor, for Act 91, budgeting, and assistance programs.
- The homeowner's own lender or servicer, for the reinstatement amount and payoff.
- The county office, to verify sale status or tax questions.

Direct phone numbers, emails, or chat handles for any teammate. See `12_not_covered.md`.
