# Not Covered (the honest list)

These are things a new setter will likely ask that this folder deliberately does not answer. For each one, the onboarder says it isn't in the folder and names who to ask. It never fills in a guess.

| New hire might ask | Not in this folder because | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| What is the percentage based on? When am I paid? | Covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`: a percentage of the assignment fee, paid at closing through title | Answer from the file |
| What makes a deal count for me? What if another setter calls the same person? | Covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`: the setter who sets the appointment that gets closed is tied to the deal, first come first served | Answer from the file |
| How is my calling time tracked? | Covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`: Go High Level or Salesforce | Answer from the file |
| Do I sign anything? | Yes, a JV (joint venture) agreement, covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`. The terms are not in this folder | Owner |
| My exact rate? Time off? Which of Go High Level or Salesforce do I use, and how do I get in? | `13_pay_and_hours.md` gives the normal 5% or sometimes 10%. The rest is not confirmed | Owner |
| How do I move up (pool to group, 5% to 10%, setter to senior setter to closer)? | Covered in `02_the_role.md` and `13_pay_and_hours.md`: no fixed thresholds, it depends on how well you handle the conversations, the role, and setting appointments, and the better you get at setting appointments the faster you move up | Answer from the file |
| How long does it usually take to move up? Who decides? | Not confirmed. Do not invent a timeline | Owner |
| How is the assignment fee split? | Covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`: setter 5% or 10%, closer 20%, the rest to the company, all from the same assignment fee | Answer from the file |
| Do closing costs or other expenses come out of my percentage? | Covered in `13_pay_and_hours.md`: closing costs are paid by the buyer and other expenses come out of the company's share | Answer from the file |
| How does a lead get marked as mine? Where do I see my follow-up list? | Ownership after rapport is covered in `02_the_role.md`. How it is marked in the system is not confirmed | Owner |
| What if my first appointment falls through and someone else books a new one? | Not confirmed. The owner has only described the case where the first setter's appointment closes | Owner |
| Am I an employee? How do I handle taxes? | Setters are independent contractors (see `13_pay_and_hours.md`). Tax and legal questions are not answered here | Owner and a tax professional of their own |
| What time do I start today, and who do I meet? | Owner sets the day | Owner |
| When do I start calling homeowners? | Set by the owner (see `01_first_day_plan.md`). Do not guess a day | Owner |
| What does my JV agreement say? | The terms are not in this folder | Owner |
| What dialer or phone system do I use? How do I log in? | System and access set by owner | Owner |
| Where do I log notes and how? | The tracking setup is set by owner | Owner |
| Can I get my login / password reset? | Never stored here | Owner |
| What's the exact script I read? | Covered in `14_setter_scripts.md` | Answer from the file |
| Who specifically takes my appointment? | Two types exist (advisor call, in-person sit-down), see `14_setter_scripts.md`. Which person takes a given one is assigned by the owner | Owner |
| How many calls or appointments should I hit? | Targets set by owner | Owner |
| What hours am I allowed to call? What are the calling rules? | The company sets no fixed call schedule. The one expectation is consistency (see `13_pay_and_hours.md`). Anything beyond that is not documented | Owner |
| Are there laws about calling or texting I need to follow? | The company's looseness about schedule is not a statement about legal rules. Legal compliance is not covered here | Owner, who can involve an attorney |
| Who is the closer on my appointments? | Assigned by owner | Owner |
| How do I reach a teammate directly? | No contact details in this folder | Owner |
| What does the second dollar amount under Debt on the county list mean? | Covered in `05_reading_the_county_list.md`: it is hand money (the winning bidder's deposit), not part of the debt | Answer from the file |
| What do I do if a homeowner sends me documents? | Not documented here | Owner |
| Will bankruptcy / a loan mod / a lawyer stop this sale? | Legal question | Licensed PA attorney, via the closer |
| Is X a good deal / what would we offer? | Offers belong to the owner | Closer or owner |

## When the answer isn't here and isn't in this list

Say: "That's not something I have, and I don't want to guess. The owner is the right person to ask." Then give the single question to ask.
