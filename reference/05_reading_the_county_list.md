# Reading the County Sheriff Sale List

**Pace note:** this is a later-in-the-week topic. Setters usually receive leads already cleaned and ranked, so they do not need to read raw county lists on day one.

Delaware County publishes sheriff sale lists as PDFs, monthly. Chester County publishes its own. The company's lead system cleans these lists before setters see them. This page explains what the raw list looks like so the new hire is not lost if they ever see one.

## Columns you will see

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| No. | The listing number for the sale, followed by a date. |
| Sold For | Blank until a sale happens. |
| Location | The municipality, then the property street address. |
| Term & No. | The court case (term and number). |
| Name | The defendant(s), usually the homeowner(s). |
| Debt | The amount owed, with a second smaller amount shown beneath it. |
| Attorney | The law firm for the plaintiff (the lender side). |

**Not confirmed in this folder:** the exact meaning of the second, smaller amount under Debt, and how to read the date next to the listing number. Do not guess. Ask the owner (see `12_not_covered.md`).

## Status notes you may see

- **STAYED**: the sale is on hold. These are often removed or deprioritized when leads are processed.
- **CONTINUED TO [date]**: the sale was pushed to a later date.
- A list header may say "Continued List for [date]." That means these are sales continued to that date.

## Name patterns that mean "review before outreach"

Some names signal a situation that needs a human look before anyone calls:

- "Estate of..." or "Deceased"
- "Known Heir" / "Unknown Heirs"
- "Administrator" or "Administratrix"
- Names that include a trust
- Companies (LLC, Inc.)
- Government names (for example the United States of America or the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development listed as a party)

In the lead tier system these go to **PENDING**: flagged for manual review. A setter does not call them until the lead has been reviewed.

## What the system tracks per lead

Sale date, stayed/continued status, amount owed, municipality, owner name, property address, whether it is owner-occupied, equity estimate, route status, knock status, follow-up status.
