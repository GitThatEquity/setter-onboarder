# Boundaries and Escalation

## A setter never

- Gives legal advice. The safe line: "I'm not an attorney, but I can help you understand the general process and point you toward the right resources."
- Promises the home can be saved or the sale can be stopped.
- Quotes a price, makes an offer, or explains a deal structure as a recommendation.
- Uses fear, shame, or pressure.
- Pretends to be from the county, the lender, the court, or a government office.
- Keeps contacting someone who asked to stop. Mark the lead Do Not Contact.
- Calls a lead flagged Pending or Manual Review before someone reviews it.
- Removes a lead permanently on their own. That needs review.
- Puts real homeowner details in a public place or an AI chat.

## When to hand off

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Homeowner asks a legal question ("will this stop the sale," "what does this document mean") | Say you're not an attorney. Point to a licensed Pennsylvania attorney, legal aid, or a HUD-approved housing counselor. Offer to connect them with a closer. |
| Homeowner wants numbers, an offer, or to discuss selling or taking over payments | Set the appointment with a closer. Do not go deep yourself. |
| Homeowner mentions an attorney or bankruptcy is involved | Don't interfere. Respect it. Note it in the log and hand to the closer. |
| Homeowner is in real distress, panicking, or mentions hurting themselves or someone else | Stop the script. Be calm and human. Tell the owner right away. Do not try to handle it alone. |
| Homeowner is angry or says to stop contacting them | Be polite. Mark Do Not Contact. Stop. |
| A lead looks like an estate, trust, heirs, probate, or a company | It belongs in Pending / Manual Review. Flag it to the owner. |
| You said something you're not sure about | Tell the owner or closer. It is better to correct early than for a homeowner to rely on it. |

## Emergencies

If a homeowner indicates they are in immediate danger, call emergency services. Do not wait on a hand-off.

## Whom to tell

See `10_whos_who.md`. When it does not name someone for a situation, the default is to tell the owner.
