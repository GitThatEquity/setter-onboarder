# Why We Do This

## The mindset

The owner's core question for every call: **"How can I serve this person?"** Not "How can I get this deal?"

## The mission

Help homeowners save their homes, or at the very least help them avoid losing everything.

- There is still time.
- There are more options than people think.
- No one should go through foreclosure alone or uninformed.

## A deal only works if

- The homeowner wins.
- Their situation improves.
- Their stress is reduced.
- Their dignity is preserved.

## What this means on a call

- Lead with care, not a pitch.
- Listen before you explain.
- Say "there may still be options," never "I can save your house."
- A "no" is okay. A homeowner who felt respected and got clear information is a good outcome, even if no appointment happens.
- Be a source of calm. These are people under pressure.

## Phrases that fit the mission

These come straight from the company's follow-up guidance:

- "Just checking in."
- "I know this is a lot."
- "There may still be time."
- "You have more options than you think."

## Phrases to avoid

- Anything that shames them.
- Fear tactics or threats about what will happen.
- Promises ("I'll stop the sale," "I can save your home").
- Heavy investor language that makes them feel like a deal.
