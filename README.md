# Setter Onboarder

An onboarding folder for a **new pre-foreclosure appointment setter** at 32 East LLC, a small real-estate company in Delaware County and Chester County, Pennsylvania. Drop it into a Claude project and Claude becomes the teammate who has been on the phones for years, likes helping, and walks a nervous new hire through day one.

## Who this is for

A new setter. They make outbound calls to Pennsylvania homeowners who are behind on their mortgage and have a sheriff sale coming. They set appointments for a closer. They may have never worked in real estate or sales.

## How to start (60 seconds)

1. Create a new Claude project.
2. Add every file in this folder to the project knowledge, keeping `reference/` together.
3. Put the contents of `identity.md` and `rules.md` in the project instructions (or upload them with the rest).
4. Tell the new hire to open a chat and say: **"Hi, it's my first day."**

## What a new hire should ask first

- "What does a setter actually do all day?"
- "What do I do first today?"
- "Where do I find my leads?"
- "What do I say when they pick up?"
- "What do I say when someone says they have it handled?"
- "How does pay work, and how many hours do I need to put in?"

The onboarder answers from the folder. When the answer is not in `reference/`, it says so and names who to ask.

## What's in the folder

| File | Job |
|---|---|
| `identity.md` | Who the onboarder is, the business, the role, what is out of scope |
| `rules.md` | How it answers, what to do when it doesn't know, how it paces day one |
| `examples.md` | Sample exchanges, including one where the answer is not in the folder |
| `reference/` | The real material the onboarder pulls from |

Inside `reference/`:

- `01_first_day_plan.md` - the day in order, and what waits until later
- `02_the_role.md` - what a setter does, what success looks like, what is not their job
- `03_why_we_do_this.md` - the mission, and the one rule behind every call
- `04_foreclosure_basics.md` - the timeline and Pennsylvania terms in plain English
- `05_reading_the_county_list.md` - what the columns on a sheriff sale list mean
- `06_lead_stages_and_tiers.md` - NOD, NOTS, Expired, and Gold / Silver / Bronze / Pending
- `07_tools_and_workflow.md` - the path a lead takes and the tools, with no logins
- `08_call_framework_and_objections.md` - the 4-step pattern and the 3 core objections
- `09_boundaries_and_escalation.md` - what a setter never does, and when to hand off
- `10_whos_who.md` - roles and what each person owns
- `11_glossary.md` - plain-English terms
- `12_not_covered.md` - the things this folder deliberately does not answer
- `14_setter_scripts.md` - the Beginner, Experienced, and Cold Call scripts
- `13_pay_and_hours.md` - how setter pay works (a percentage of the assignment fee, paid at closing) and the 10-hour weekly calling minimum

## About the details

All homeowner examples are invented. No real leads, phone numbers, logins, or pay figures are in this repo. Where a detail depends on the owner (exact pay rate, dialer, team contacts), `reference/12_not_covered.md` and `reference/10_whos_who.md` mark it as "owner fills in" so the onboarder never guesses.

## Testing it (do this before handing it over)

Play a nervous new hire for three rounds:

1. Ask something the folder covers ("What's a NOTS?").
2. Ask something it does not ("What's my commission?", "What's the dialer password?"). It must say it doesn't know and point to a person.
3. Ask for a legal opinion ("Will bankruptcy stop the sale?"). It must decline to advise and route to a licensed attorney.
